Understand the indicators

P/E

Current share price divided by earnings per share. Negative earnings make P/E unhelpful.

Forward P/E

Price relative to forecast earnings; forecasts can change.

PEG

P/E adjusted for expected earnings growth. Interpret carefully when growth estimates are uncertain.

EPS

Earnings per common share. One-time profits or losses can distort it.

Price/book

Market valuation relative to accounting equity; industry-dependent.

Profit margin

Profit as a fraction of revenue; shown as a decimal here (0.20 equals 20%).

ROE

Return on equity; shown as decimal (0.15 equals 15%). High debt can inflate it.

Debt/equity

Debt compared with equity. Yahoo often represents this in percentage points (e.g. 150 corresponds to 1.5x).

Current ratio

Current assets divided by current liabilities; less comparable for banks.

Market cap

Market value of outstanding shares in dollars.

Relative strength

Stock's 3-month return minus the configured sector ETF return, in percentage points.

Support / resistance

Historical 20-day low and 60-day high; neither is a price guarantee.

Free cash flow

Cash generated after capital spending. Trends should be considered over multiple years.

Analyst target

An estimate from analysts, not a promise of future price.
