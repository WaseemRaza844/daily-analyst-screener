How to interpret the numbers

P/E: Price divided by earnings per share. PEG: P/E relative to expected earnings growth. EPS: Profit per share. Forward P/E: Valuation using projected earnings. ROE: Net profitability compared with shareholder equity. Debt/equity: Leverage; industry norms differ. Free cash flow: Cash available after capital spending. Support and resistance: Past price levels, not guaranteed trading zones.
